🏖 Diani Beach

Kenya's Award-Winning Tropical Paradise

About Diani Beach

Diani Beach is located on Kenya's south coast, approximately 30 km south of Mombasa. It stretches for nearly 17 kilometers along the Indian Ocean and is famous for its powder-white sand, warm turquoise waters, palm trees, and luxury resorts. It has won several international awards as one of Africa's best beach destinations.
