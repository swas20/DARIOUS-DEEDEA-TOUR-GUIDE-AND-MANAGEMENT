⛵ Lamu Island

Experience Kenya's Rich Swahili Heritage

About Lamu Island

Lamu Island is Kenya's oldest continually inhabited town and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is famous for its rich Swahili culture, ancient architecture, dhow sailing, peaceful beaches, and narrow streets where donkeys remain the main mode of transport.

History

Founded over 700 years ago, Lamu developed as an important trading center connecting Africa, Arabia, India, and Asia. Today it preserves its unique Swahili traditions, historic buildings, and cultural festivals.
